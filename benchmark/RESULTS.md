## Caveats

- Probabilities are calibrated to this dataset's mix (about one attack per two benign rows). Real traffic has far fewer attacks, so treat the score as a ranking and the thresholds as review cut-offs.
- Attacks come from dnstwist and a small curated list; benign rows from the OpenDNS top 10,000, which may itself contain a few look-alikes.
- ASCII typos of brands that are not in `brands.json` are out of scope (nothing to compare with).
